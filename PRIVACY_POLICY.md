# Privacy Policy for ScanLite

**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

## Overview
ScanLite is an offline QR code scanner and generator. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection
ScanLite does **NOT** collect, store, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- **No internet connection required** - the app works completely offline
- **No analytics or tracking** - we do not use any analytics tools
- **No user accounts** - no sign-up or login required
- **No personal information** - we do not collect names, emails, or any identifiers

## Camera Permission
ScanLite requests camera access solely to scan QR codes in real-time. Camera data is processed on-device only and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

## Local Storage
ScanLite stores scan history locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Can be cleared at any time from the app settings
- Is automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled

## Third-Party Services
ScanLite does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs.

## Children's Privacy
ScanLite does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
