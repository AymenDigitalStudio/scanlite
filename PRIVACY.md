# Privacy Policy

**ScanLite — Offline QR Scanner**

Last updated: September 2026

## Overview

ScanLite is an offline QR and barcode scanner. Your privacy is our priority.

## Data Collection

ScanLite does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

- **Camera access** is used solely for scanning QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera data is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
- **Gallery access** is used solely to read images you select for QR code scanning. Images are never uploaded or stored by the app.
- **Scan history** is stored entirely on your device using local storage. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any server or third party.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Camera | Live QR/barcode scanning |
| Internet | Opening scanned URLs (only when user taps "Open") |

## Third-Party Services

ScanLite does **not** use any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app's About section.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
